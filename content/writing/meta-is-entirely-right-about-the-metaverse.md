The Metaverse. Does Zuck really think that we want to replace our reality with 1s and 0s? I think not entirely, and here is why.

While you may see the Metaverse as some dystopian subversion of the way life currently operates (or a logical extension of the environment created by the pandemic); I believe that it is in fact neither of those things, and it could very well be the best solution for problems that we are yet to confront — but are just around the corner.

Allow me to introduce a concept. The concept of a “digital twin”. Over the course of my current semester, I conducted research on a field known as NeRF (neural radiance fields). Essentially, by capturing discrete 2D image data, a continuous 3D reconstruction of a scene can be “learned” by a neural network. This, in essence, captures a “digital twin." We, as humans, perceive and process representations of information. The 1s and 0s that tell your display what dot to contrast as compared to another dot are what are allowing you to read this text right now.

A picture looks like its subject, but it is not in itself its subject.

But it's all the same to us.

In the advent of movie theaters, people were afraid of motion pictures, afraid that the train projected on the screen in front of them was truly going to crash into them.

![The scene of some of the first people to watch motion pictures](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/480/1*2xAmn2rNr3ldFGsQyOZs_Q.png)

Now where am I going with all of this.

In this age of information, it is natural that information is king. We have gone about our lives taking photos of ourselves, recording our voices, and relaying our likeness freely and openly on the World Wide Web. But now what we have done is constructed of ourselves a “digital twin." With the right technology, your likeness can be fully replicated into lifelike representations of the real you.

This is a **major** problem.

Your voice can be made to say things that you never said.

Your face can be grafted into situations you were never in.

Your digital twin can wreak havoc on your life in abhorrent and malicious ways.

I believe the Metaverse is the solution to this incoming information disaster. By acting as a buffer between your true likeness and your digital likeness, you are free to share your thoughts and spread your ideas while your true self is shielded from potential bad actors.

Now, while I am unsure of whether Mr. Zuckerberg and his team had this in mind when conceptualizing the Metaverse, I do still believe that the Metaverse itself is a necessary panacea for a coming information storm, where truth and untruth meld into one incoherent fog.
