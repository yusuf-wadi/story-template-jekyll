Everything has become compartmentalized,  
Individualized, a product.  
Nothing more than to be packaged and sold under 100 different names in 100 different ways.  
Nothing is connected anymore, everything must stand on its own, have its own identity.  
Everything must be for its own sake.   
We run in place for the sake of running.  
Lift heavy blocks for the sake of lifting.  
Spending more, to have more to spend on.  
Relationships purely for the sake of seeing ourselves in relationships.  
Pleasure for the sake of pleasure.  
Information for the sake of itself, creation for the sake of itself.  
We root everything back into our selves, wondering why we can’t grow.

_Infinite Power Glitch?_

We think we found unlimited power in our delusions, true independence.  
But in reality, we have provided easy targets for those who seek to control us.  
The wolf preys on the lone sheep.

A self completing cycle.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*nSI2cwrkEaf23536a1Jf9A.jpeg)

_Ouroboros_

Circular reasoning, a self defeating argument.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/900/1*0rQYhPa7C1P20O6dpkS7HA.jpeg)

_hamster wheel of life — keiross_

Every little concept is taken, lobotomized, carved, and pushed to its limits.  
Pure hedonism and debauchery.

We’ve forgotten the inherent synergies of nature.

The interconnected nature of it all

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/464/1*5mUECitE0zHHb4uinHU_2w.jpeg)

_fractals_

We slam the gas on ideas seeing them as separate, but in reality its all the same.  
Everything abides by the same laws, though expressed differently.  
Just as everything I have typed abides by the same grammar, just expressed in different ways.

As individuals carve themselves out seeking to differentiate, the intricate marble of society crumbles into sand, to be blown away.

The wolf preys on the lone sheep.

Loneliness is a trillion dollar industry.

We must resist the call to individualism, reconnect and rekindle.   
Re-foster and rebuild.  
Reconnecting individuals into families.  
Families into communities.  
And communities into the human race, acting as one unit, responding as one being to the challenges placed before us.
